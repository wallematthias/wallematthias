## Research Software

I build practical HR-pQCT and bone-imaging software: reproducible analysis
pipelines, Slicer-facing tools, mechanics solvers, and simulation/validation
workflows.

| Area | Repositories | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Longitudinal HR-pQCT | [TimelapsedHRpQCT](https://github.com/wallematthias/TimelapsedHRpQCT), [SlicerTimelapsedHRpQCT](https://github.com/wallematthias/SlicerTimelapsedHRpQCT) | Pairwise registration, compartment masks, remodelling maps, and Slicer workflow integration. |
| Mechanoregulation | [BoneMechanoregulation](https://github.com/wallematthias/BoneMechanoregulation) | Downstream surface-based SED/remodelling analysis with Schulte-style curves, lazy-zone thresholds, and OR summaries. |
| Mechanics | [parosol-py](https://github.com/wallematthias/parosol-py) | Python-accessible finite-element mechanics and scanner/profile shortcuts for HR-pQCT-style analyses. |
| Simulation | `AdvectionRemodelling` | Advection-based synthetic bone remodelling examples and mini-bone benchmark data. |
| Motion and image QA | [MotionScoreHRpQCT](https://github.com/wallematthias/MotionScoreHRpQCT), [SlicerMotionScoreHRpQCT](https://github.com/wallematthias/SlicerMotionScoreHRpQCT), [hrpqct-geodesic-contour](https://github.com/wallematthias/hrpqct-geodesic-contour) | Motion scoring, contour support, and image quality tooling. |
| MRI bone analysis | [BoneQuantMRI](https://github.com/wallematthias/BoneQuantMRI) | MATLAB-based ROI segmentation and phantom calibration for MRI bone images. |

Current public stack: `TimelapsedHRpQCT` -> `BoneMechanoregulation` with
`parosol-py` providing the mechanics layer. Slicer wrappers support interactive
review and workflow integration.
